# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the mlx-one community respectful and
harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or
level of technical expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Welcome questions and differing technical perspectives.
- Focus feedback on ideas and observable behavior, not people.
- Respect privacy, licenses, dataset terms, and model usage restrictions.

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, threats, and publication of private
information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns
privately using the security contact in `SECURITY.md`. Maintainers will review
reports promptly and protect reporter privacy where possible.
